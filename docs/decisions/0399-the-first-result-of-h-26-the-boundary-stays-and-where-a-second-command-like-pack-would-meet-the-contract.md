# 0399. The first result of H-26: the boundary stays, and where a second command-like pack would meet the contract

**Status:** Accepted. Design decision, made under [decision 0294](0294-the-owner-hands-the-open-questions-to-the-agents.md). The owner had not read this record when it was merged.

**Date:** 2026-11-24

**Settles:** the question [decision 0099](0099-the-command-pack-as-building-it-settled-it.md) left to package D4: whether the one kernel change the command pack forced calls for redrawing the boundary between kernel and packs. **Changes:** the status of hypothesis H-26 in the register; the roadmap and the build order mark D3 and D4. **Result file:** [H-26-result.md](../hypotheses/H-26-result.md).

## Decision

1. **The boundary is not redrawn.** Counted from the records, the command pack forced one change that altered the plan's shape ([decision 0124](0124-the-plan-declares-a-surfaces-programs-and-a-schema-checks-run-time-values.md)), asked for or forced five additions with another pack or an audit, and was one of every pack asked for four more. All but the first are methods with defaults, a function moved into the contract, or a new constructor. None names a mode, a surface kind, or the pack. By the roadmap's rule (more than slightly) and section 306's rule (more than two of its seven likely changes), this is slight: one of seven.
2. **H-26 is `supported`, with limits.** The limits are written in the result: the count rests on what each record says a change was for; a pack that needs several findings at once to draft a tool is not shown; the other kinds' shared-name and held-folder changes are counted in the register's dated notes, not in this result.
3. **Where a second pack that starts programs would meet the contract first.** The supervisor's one-shot link: one byte bound for both streams, output beyond it dropped until the program ends, no grace period, and no count of starts at once. Decision 0099 listed these as the command pack's own workarounds, and a second pack that starts programs would have to repeat them. When one is built, these go into the supervisor once, before the second copy. This record does not decide that.

## What was weighed instead

- **Redraw the boundary now,** as 0099 allowed, by moving the program check and the program-start gaps into the kernel as a "starts programs" layer. Rejected: the check of programs already moved into the provider contract ([decision 0250](0250-one-check-of-who-may-change-a-program-and-the-mcp-packs-local-server-on-windows.md)) when the second pack needed it, which is the cheapest time to move shared code; a layer made before a second user is a guess about its shape.
- **Leave H-26 open until every pack has been counted.** Rejected: the register's other dated notes already carry the other packs; this hypothesis is answered for the pack it names, and the remaining limit is written where a reader will find it.

## What would reopen it

A second pack that starts programs needing a kernel change that names what a program is; or a pack of a third archetype that cannot be added without a plan change as large as decision 0124's.

## What this inferred

The owner has not spoken to this point; the settled reading is that the owner's direction on a neutral kernel (the long-term plan, stage D) is met by "few and general", not "none". Said so, so it can be corrected.

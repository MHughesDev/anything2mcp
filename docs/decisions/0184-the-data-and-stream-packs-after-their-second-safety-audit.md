# 0184. The data and stream packs after their second safety audit

**Status:** Accepted. Design decision. The owner had not read this record when it was merged.

**Date:** 2026-11-17

**Changes:** the drivers, the PostgreSQL store, the NATS client, and the drafting of [`a2m-pack-data`](../architecture/crates/a2m-pack-data.md) and [`a2m-pack-stream`](../architecture/crates/a2m-pack-stream.md); the stands `targets/example-db` and `targets/example-nats`; sentences of spec sections [307](../spec/3-packs/307-the-data-pack.md) and [309](../spec/3-packs/309-the-stream-pack.md). Takes up the two open points of [decision 0182](0182-the-file-data-and-stream-packs-after-the-first-drafted-reads.md) for the stream pack.

## Decision

A reviewer read both packs against their documents and the ten rules, and ran nothing. The lead ruled on each finding that needed a choice. Each finding was first shown against a stand by a test that fails before the fix, or refuted by the code.

| Finding | Verdict | Fix | Alternative weighed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1, critical: a kept connection reused without checking where it leads | Confirmed: with the surface moved to a second database, the next run of the session wrote to the first (`a_kept_connection_never_serves_a_surface_that_now_leads_to_another_database` fails without the fix) | The pool's key holds the surface's name, the session, the epoch, and a `Leads`: address and port dialled, database, schema, link mode, role. At `take` (each `prepare`, and each writer's `begin`) a connection's own `Leads` is compared with the plan's, and a connection under the same surface and session that leads elsewhere is closed. Before every transaction the server is asked `current_database()`, `session_user`, `current_user`, `search_path`, `standard_conforming_strings`, and its own address and port; any difference stops the step unsent with `connection_leads_elsewhere`, and the connection is closed. The embedded store keeps no handle between runs; its file's `FileIdentity` is taken at `prepare` and every connection opened by the name is held to it (`store_file_replaced`) | Comparing the server's own address and port with the surface's, as the ruling read. Refused: a server behind a relay (the stand's), a container's port mapping, or a local socket reports another port or none, so the check would refuse every such setup while proving nothing a dialled address does not. The server's report is held instead to what it said when the connection opened |
| P2, serious: a `--` comment ended only at a line feed | Confirmed against the real server: `SELECT 1 AS a --\r, current_setting('search_path') AS b` prepares with two columns (`a_comment_the_server_ends_at_a_carriage_return_is_refused_before_it_is_sent`) | Every control character but a line feed and a tab is refused anywhere in a statement's text, in both dialects, by the compiler and the driver; the server dialect also ends a comment at a carriage return. `search_path` is read again before every transaction (P1) | Refusing them only outside string literals, as the ruling allows. Refused: a check that reads no literal cannot be wrong about where one ends, and a literal that needs a control character can be a bound parameter |
| N1, serious: a write in doubt reported as a refused session | Confirmed with the stand's new faults `ExpireAuthAfterStore` and `ExpireAuthAfterAck` | Once a publish or an acknowledgement has left, every `-ERR` is `Unexpected` with an unknown fate, so the driver reports `possible_commit`; an `-ERR` names a refused session only at `CONNECT` or during a look | Mapping only the authentication words to doubt. Refused: nothing ties any `-ERR` to the request in flight |
| N2, serious: one message taken by two records | Confirmed: with the consumer's wait run out, a second engine took and acknowledged the message a first record held (`a_message_delivered_again_is_never_taken_by_a_second_record`) | A delivery's count comes from its acknowledgement subject, a declared consumer's `ack_wait` from its description. The driver marks, for the engine's life, each message a take handed out: `held` from the record until its acknowledgement is answered or a `taken` look settles it, `given_back` when no record holds it. A take stops with `message_may_be_held` on a `held` message, and on one delivered before unless it is `given_back`. An acknowledgement that would be sent later than the consumer's wait less one second is not sent, and the record is in doubt. A take holds its consumer against every other run of the engine (`subscription_in_use`, unsent). The stand redelivers after each consumer's wait on its clock, and already removes a message on an acknowledgement of any delivery | Looking up the record that took the message first, as the ruling asks. Not possible: the provider contract gives a driver its own record's recorded values only. The engine-life mark is the pack's part; the lookup is owed by the contract (below). The cost, named in the design: after an engine restart, a message delivered before is not taken until a person settles or removes it |
| N3, serious: "absent" said of a log that loses messages from inside | Confirmed with a stream that keeps two messages a subject, and with a message deleted by hand (`a_scan_never_says_absent_of_a_log_that_lost_a_message_from_inside`) | The channel's facts carry `may_lose_inside`, from the stream's configuration read at the step: a limit per subject other than none or one, `allow_msg_ttl`, or retention other than `limits`. A scan says "absent" only where that is false, a `scan_from` was recorded before the publish, and the range from it to the log's end was read whole, every position present. Otherwise `scan_inconclusive`, and the record stays in doubt. The pack never relies on the duplicate window to say "absent" | Relying on an open duplicate window. Refused for now: the pack cannot tell the time between the publish and the look without a clock the record keeps |
| N4, serious: the server's `max_payload` sizes the client's buffer | Confirmed by reading: a stated limit near the top of `u64` overflows the sum; the unit test with a fake server stops cleanly now | The setting `message_ceiling_bytes` (default 8 MiB, at most 64 MiB) bounds every message; a server's limit above it is held to it; a larger stated size stops before anything is set aside, with checked sums; the buffer grows as bytes arrive | A fixed 64 MiB. Refused: eight times the server's default is enough for a message of a tool, and a setting lets a person who needs more say so |
| N5, minor | Confirmed with the stand's fault `ForeignDelivery` | A delivery whose acknowledgement names another stream or consumer than the one pulled is `unexpected_reply` | — |
| N6, minor | Confirmed | A channel under `$` or `_INBOX` does not compile and is refused by the driver: `place()` refuses it | Listing the known prefixes only. Refused: anything under `$` is the server's |
| N7, minor | Confirmed | A binding's header whose name begins `Nats-`, in any case, does not compile; the token header is compared without case; the driver refuses both again | Allowing the harmless ones. Refused: the set the server reads grows with its versions |
| N8, minor | Confirmed by reading | A stream or consumer name the server lists is held to `is_name`, a subject to `is_subject` | — |
| N9, minor | Confirmed by reading | At most 1000 messages of a topic are held while something else is read; more is `unexpected_reply` | — |
| P3, minor | Confirmed | A server surface's address must be among the run's destinations (`surface_not_declared`) | — |
| P4, minor | Confirmed with the stand's new relay fault `HoldCommitAnswer` | `COMMIT` waits until the step's deadline; then the write is in doubt and the connection is closed. Nothing is cancelled | Sending a cancel. Refused: a cancel cut short is the doubt itself |
| P5, minor | Confirmed, not reachable | Closed by a sentence in the design: the driver never commits a transaction that failed, so the tag `ROLLBACK` cannot arrive. Reading the tag needs a client call the library does not offer | — |
| P6, minor | Confirmed | A member of `pg_read_server_files`, `pg_write_server_files`, `pg_execute_server_program`, `pg_signal_backend`, `pg_read_all_data`, `pg_write_all_data`, `pg_monitor`, `pg_read_all_settings`, or `pg_read_all_stats` is `identity_is_administrator` | — |
| 0182's open points: the `role` question, and a list's fields | Done | A channel file's `role` is optional; no drafted channel asks it; a publish over a channel with none does not compile and asks. A `list` gives each field of its messages as a list in their order, beside `count` and `more`, which no field takes over; the drafted list outputs them, from the schema or else from the fields a look saw with one type (`sampled_fields`) | Leaving the question in the file with a default. Refused: a default `role` would make a publish an ordinary write or not without anyone deciding |

## Consequences

- **Owed by the provider contract:** a way for a driver to ask whether another record of the same operation recorded a given value (the stream sequence a take recorded), so that a message delivered again after an engine restart can be taken once that record is settled, as the ruling wanted.
- **Owed by the scheduler and the action registry:** the stream mode's `exclusive = "link"` is to hold two engines apart on one consumer; the driver holds runs of one engine apart only.
- **Owed by the engine:** a list output normalized value by value (decision 0182); the stream pack's test helper does what the engine should.
- After an engine restart, a queue whose messages were delivered before and not taken stops every take on them until a person settles the record or removes the message. That is safe and visible; the lookup above removes it.

## Revisit if

The contract gives a driver the lookup above; or a record keeps a time a lookup can trust, so that an open duplicate window could let a scan say "absent".

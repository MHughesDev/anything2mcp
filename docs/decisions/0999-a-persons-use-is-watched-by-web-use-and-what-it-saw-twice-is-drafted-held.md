# 0999. A person's use is watched by `web_use`, and what it saw twice in one shape is drafted, held

**Status:** Accepted. Design decision, made by the worker who built package E2, under the lead's brief. The owner had not read this record when it was merged. It changes no public interface: the web probe's manifest gains a discoverer, and the `exchange` and `endpoint` findings gain names a capture fills.

**Date:** 2026-11-25

**Changes:** [`a2m-pack-web-probe`](../architecture/crates/a2m-pack-web-probe.md), which gains the discoverer `web_use` (`watch.rs`), a body on its `Exchange` and on its endpoints, and the profile `watching`; [`a2m-pack-web-ui`](../architecture/crates/a2m-pack-web-ui.md), whose drafting rules from an `exchange` (`draft_request.rs`) and from a `screen` (`draft.rs`) learn what a capture gives, and whose page request kind says that a held request with its effect asked writes nothing; the test application, which gains a person who walks its pages (`person.rs`). It builds points 1, 3, and 4 of [decision 0392](0392-a-page-that-reads-by-a-method-that-may-write-is-learned-from-a-persons-watched-use-and-read-through-an-asserted-request.md) on the capture job of [decision 0433](0433-a-capture-is-asked-for-by-capture-start-begun-by-its-person-on-the-page-and-held-in-the-engines-memory.md), with the correction of [decision 0394](0394-an-asserted-read-of-a-web-surface-is-a-page-request-matched-by-one-shared-shape-and-shown-at-publish.md) for what is drafted, and settles what they left to the builder.

## Context

Decision 0392 says what a capture of a person's use records, what it is evidence of, and what is drafted from a request by a method that may write. Decision 0433 built the job that calls a person-driven discoverer, and package E2c the watching tab. Left to this package: which profile the watching tab opens in, which page a request is put to, how "seen at least twice with one shape" reaches the drafter, what the held page request holds, how every draft from a capture ends up in the holding folder, and how a test stands for the person.

## Decision

1. **`web_use` opens one watching tab and reads it only through the contract.** Called by `DiscovererHandle::watch`, it opens a watching tab on the surface's own address, once that address passes the check every address of a discovery passes, and sends nothing after that. It takes the tab's snapshots and its requests, the requests only through `take_capture`, each time the tab has taken the snapshot of a screen, and once more when the capture ends or the person closes the window. Its `discover` is refused as `policy`/`effect_not_declared`, which the handle already says before it is called. Its bound is 1800 seconds, the longest a capture runs.
2. **The watching tab opens in a profile of its own, `watching`.** A capture's scope names no person (decision 0395 left `DiscoveryScope` as it was), so the tab cannot open in the profile the person signed in with. A person who must sign in to the surface signs in in the watching window, and that profile keeps the session for the next capture. It is never the profile of discovery, which is signed in to nothing, nor of the person's sign-in window.
3. **A request is put to the screen the person was on when it was taken.** The tab takes a screen's snapshot once its own requests have ended, so the requests taken right after a snapshot are that screen's. A request sent as the person moved on, such as a save followed at once by a move, may be put to the screen they came to; every `exchange` finding says so in a limitation. The tab knows which document sent each request and does not say; the base giving that is the better design, and is left to a later package of the base.
4. **How often, and in how many shapes, is in the finding.** Requests of one method to one path from one page are one `exchange` finding with `seen`, the count, and `shapes`, how many shapes of body and of answer they came in. Reads by key over pages that differ in one segment are matched first, as `web_capture` matches them (decision 0190). The finding keeps the body as `body`: its media type, its fields' names and kinds, and whether names were withheld. Endpoints are inferred by the functions `web_capture` uses, and an endpoint whose requests sent a body gains `request` and `request_media_types`; one of reads by `GET` keeps the form it had. An endpoint with a placeholder is `inferred` (decision 0392, point 3).
5. **The drafter reads `seen` and `shapes`.** A finding that says neither is one of exploration and is drafted as before. One that says them is drafted only when `seen` is at least two and `shapes` is one; otherwise it is listed as not drafted with the reason (decision 0308, point 3). A request by a method other than `GET` is drafted only from a finding that says them.
6. **The held page request.** For a request by `POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH`, or `DELETE`, answered with a success in JSON, whose body is JSON or a form with every name kept: `page_requests/{words}.yaml` with the method, the path, the page that sent it, `effect` as the question of decision 0392, point 4, in its words, `request_encoding`, a `request_schema` that names each top-level field with its kind and closes with `additionalProperties: false`, and the answer's shape with the question of what it always holds. No `enum`, no `read_asserted`, no value, and no operation. A body whose names were withheld, a field of more than one kind, or a field named as a secret is not drafted, with the reason: a closed schema that left a field out would be one the page's own request fails.
7. **A held request with its effect asked writes nothing.** The page request kind tells the drafter whether an entry writes. It now answers no for an entry by a method other than `GET` whose `effect` is an open question and that has no `read_asserted`: such an entry is held, the loader reads nothing in the holding folder, and it reads or writes only once a person writes its effect. Any other entry by such a method is still a write the drafter never writes.
8. **Every draft from a capture is held.** A `screen` finding of `web_use` says `seen_by: person`. The screens drafted from it ask, in place of `ready`, what shows the page is ready when the engine opens it, because the engine never opened it; the reads over them are held with them. Reads by `GET` are held by the question their answer already asks.
9. **The test application stands for the person.** A test sets a walk through `POST /__control/person/walk`; while one is set, every page carries a script that takes its steps in order across pages, kept in the tab's session storage: go to a path, wait for a text, linger, type, submit, send a JSON body. It is the form the sign-in window's test already takes, `person_at_the_window` (package 2.3). Nothing of the engine types, presses, or navigates.
10. **Drafting from a capture has no command yet.** `endpoints.infer` still has no handler, and it belongs to `a2m-actions`. The proof drafts the findings `finding.describe` gives with the drafter of the engine's own registry, which is what a handler would do; a scout's draft stage does the same with its own findings.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Put each request to the screen whose snapshot comes next | A screen's own load requests would be put to the screen before it, and a read by key would never match its page |
| Take the requests at every change of the page's address | A new document's script starts its requests before the change is heard, so they would be put to the page left |
| Add the sending document's address to the capture contract now | It changes `CapturedRequest`, a public contract, and the base; this package holds neither, and the screen rule is right for the reads a capture drafts |
| Mark a screen of a capture held by a new key the loader reads | `ready` is a key the screen has, and its true answer is the person's; a new key would be a second way to hold a draft |
| Draft the held request with an empty `request_schema` | Decision 0394 lets an asserted read compile only with a closed body; the person would have to write it whole |
| Drive the watching tab from the test through the base's test support | The tab is held inside the probe's discoverer in the product; only a page can act as the person there, as the sign-in window's test already does |

## Proof

`a_person_s_use_is_captured_in_a_watching_tab_and_drafted_held`, in `crates/anything2mcp/tests/capture_use.rs`, on a virtual screen: an agent asks with `capture start`, the person begins it on the engine's page, the test application's person walks its pages in the watching window, and `capture stop` ends it. Drafted from what `finding list` and `finding describe` give: the read by `GET` two orders' summaries made, held, as a page request and a read; the search behind `POST` seen twice, held, as a page request whose effect is the question, whose body is closed, with no value, no `enum`, no `read_asserted`, and no read; the save stored once and drafted as nothing, said as seen once; nothing outside the holding folder; the application heard the address the engine opened and what the person did, and nothing else; a planted credential, typed into the form and naming a body's field, is in nothing a command printed and no file under the engine's home or the project. The drafting rules alone are proven in `crates/a2m-pack-web-ui/tests/captured.rs`.

## Revisit if

A capture of FT6 puts a request to the wrong page often enough to draft a wrong page request; then the base gives the sending document's address, through the capture contract. Or a person must capture while signed in as themself, in the profile of their sign-in; then the scope carries the capture's person.

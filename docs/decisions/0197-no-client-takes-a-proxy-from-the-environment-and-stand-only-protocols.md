## A proxy a person sets

There is none today. The installation setting `network.proxy` is in the settings schema and nothing reads it, so a value there changes nothing: every connection is direct. A proxy would be: named in that protected setting only, never taken from a variable; handed to `tls::client_builder` as an argument, so the one maker still decides; refused for a plain `http` destination that carries a credential, unless the proxy itself is on this machine; and shown by `doctor.run` with where it leads. Until it is built, a non-empty `network.proxy` should stop the engine at start rather than be ignored (owed by the settings' owner).

## Consequences

- The workspace's clients, the OAuth exchange included, never read `HTTP_PROXY`, `HTTPS_PROXY`, `ALL_PROXY`, `NO_PROXY`, or the system's proxy settings. A person behind a proxy that is the only way out cannot reach an outside service until `network.proxy` is built.
- A broker surface at `localhost:4222` is refused; it must be `127.0.0.1:4222` or `[::1]:4222`.
- The stand `targets/example-broker` depends on `a2m-pack-stream` (a development cycle, which Cargo allows); `Cargo.lock` gains that line.
- The answer of `recorded_elsewhere` is `CannotTell` until the engine passes an answerer through `DriverHandle::act_asking`; until then the cost decision 0184 named stands. The engine's answerer must leave out a take whose record was settled as not taken (its `taken` look found the message still there), or a message delivered again would wait for ever.
- The contract has no way to ask while a driver's state is borrowed from its `Prepared`: the stream driver re-borrows its run around the question. A handle the driver could take out first (`Prepared::asker()`) would be simpler; it is a contract change for its owner.

## Revisit if

The engine answers `recorded_elsewhere`; `network.proxy` is built; or the test broker's protocol is needed outside the tests.

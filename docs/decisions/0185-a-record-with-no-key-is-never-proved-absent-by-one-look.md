# 0185. A record with no key is never proved absent by one look

**Status:** Accepted. Design decision. The owner had not read this record when it was merged.

**Date:** 2026-11-17

**Changes:** the designs of [`a2m-engine`](../architecture/crates/a2m-engine.md), [`a2m-actions`](../architecture/crates/a2m-actions.md), [`a2m-provider`](../architecture/crates/a2m-provider.md), [`a2m-ledger`](../architecture/crates/a2m-ledger.md), and [`a2m-core`](../architecture/crates/a2m-core.md); sentences of spec sections [201](../spec/2-kernel/201-archetypes-and-modes.md) and [208](../spec/2-kernel/208-runs-the-ledger-and-resume.md), of the [technical reference](../reference/technical-reference.md), and the `target_versions` table of the [ledger schema](../reference/ledger-schema.md). Adds to the provider manifest, a public contract, the field `absence_steps`. Supersedes the row "A record of no identity in doubt" of [decision 0176](0176-the-action-registry-takes-up-the-kernels-second-pass.md) and the bound of `said` in [decision 0162](0162-the-provider-contracts-second-pass-what-a-step-is-handed-and-hands-back.md) point 4.

## Context

The second safety pass over the kernel found that a write of a record with no key, whose answer was lost, went back to `planned` on the first "absent" any read-back gave, and was sent again (E-1, critical); that such a record, found, was marked verified on the intended digest with nothing compared (E-3); that it was read by what was left of values the redactor had masked (E-2); that a reconcile was given the values an earlier attempt recorded (A-1); that the approval page showed the evidence of a binding chosen at preview, which the engine never held the writing binding to (E-4); that a record ended on an acknowledgement gave no outputs on resume (E-5); and that what the system said was cut to 400 characters and its line breaks flattened before any redactor saw it (C-1).

## Decision

| Finding | Confirmed by | Built | Alternative weighed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E-1 | `a_keyless_record_its_read_back_finds_absent_stays_in_doubt_and_is_not_sent_again` (the old test turned around: the stand's write count stays one) | For a record of identity `none`, "absent" is never proof by itself. A read-back's "absent" proves nothing. The record returns to `planned` only when the binding's lookup is made of step kinds its mode's manifest lists in `absence_steps` (new, default empty; `DriverHandle::proves_absence`), asked once `settle_ms` has passed since the ledger recorded what followed the send (`RecordSnapshot::sent_by`; with nothing recorded, `intent_at` plus the time the act steps may take), and it answers "absent" twice, the second after another `settle_ms`. The lease must cover both waits and both looks, or the job ends with the record in doubt. Every other case stops with `possible_commit`, `cannot_reconcile`, showing what was recorded, and nothing is sent. Tests: `a_keyless_record_goes_back_to_planned_only_when_an_authoritative_lookup_answers_absent_twice`, `..._finds_on_its_second_look_is_compared_and_verified`, `a_keyless_lookup_is_not_asked_before_the_settle_time_has_passed_since_the_send` | Trusting a read-back that a binding marks as covering everything: a read-back is the binding's word, not the provider's, and no driver checks it |
| E-3 | `a_keyless_record_found_with_other_values_is_a_mismatch_and_never_verified`; the found case failed too, as `field_not_read_back`, once values were compared, so the stand's look now returns the order it read | What a look finds is compared field by field after normalization: equal is `verified` on the digest of what was read; different is `mismatch`, `read_back_differs`, never settled, left in doubt, so nothing sends it again. A binding whose evidence is `acknowledged` that a look finds stays in doubt with what was found shown: migration 0015's triggers end a record on an acknowledgement only from `acted` under the sending lease, and a lost answer leaves it `failed` | Settling a mismatch as `stale` through `Reconciled::Neither`: the next preview of a keyless create is a create again, so a new approval would send it a second time |
| E-2 | `a_keyless_record_whose_recorded_values_were_masked_is_not_read_by_what_is_left` (two recorded values, one masked) | The refusal `read_in_doubt` makes: a record with a masked recorded value is not read at all, and stops for a person | Reading by the remainder and trusting only "found": what is found by part of the values may be another record |
| A-1 | `a_record_in_doubt_is_read_by_the_values_its_own_attempt_recorded` (action registry, real store) | The runner takes a record's values recorded before its save only from `need` events after its current intent (`RecordRow::intent_seq`, new, with `intent_at`); the engine clears them when a keyed record returns to `planned`; the engine's test control drops them at each new intent | A column of the record's attempt on each `need` event: a migration, for what the intent's event number already orders |
| E-4 | `a_write_through_another_binding_than_the_approved_one_is_not_sent`, `a_record_whose_binding_is_chosen_later_is_shown_with_every_binding_it_could_take` | Each diff record names the binding that writes it (`DiffRecord::binding`: position, mode, evidence), in the hash; a record whose key waits for an earlier record names every binding it could take (`could_take`), and its `evidence` is the weakest of theirs, so the ledger and the page treat it as possibly acknowledged. The runner hands the stored diff back (`RunSnapshot::approved`, its hash checked against the run's); at the commit point, before the lock and the intent, a binding the approval does not name stops the run with `policy`, `approval_stale`, unsent. The page gets `PreviewRecord::bindings` and `binding_later`, and `MAY_NOT_BE_READ_BACK` where only some bindings are acknowledged | Comparing only the evidence of the two bindings: a binding with the same evidence and another mode or surface is still not what was approved |
| E-5 | `the_outputs_of_a_record_ended_on_an_acknowledgement_are_handed_back_on_resume` (before: the run ended `failed`, `input`) | `adopt` gives an acknowledged record its outputs from what its `acted` mark stored, as a verified record's; in the job that sends it, its outputs are the same values, its steps up to and with the save; a confirm step's values are evidence only. The engine's test control now ends the record through `Store::store_acknowledged`, as the product's does | Storing the outputs again with the acknowledgement: a change of `Control::acknowledged`, a public interface, for values the `acted` mark already holds |
| C-1 | `a_secret_of_several_lines_or_across_the_display_cut_is_redacted_whole` | `Failure::with_said` keeps the text whole up to `SAID_CEILING` (64 KiB); above it `SAID_TOO_LONG` stands in its place, never a prefix. The handle's redactor sees the whole text. Doors cut it after redaction with `said_cut` / `said_shown` at `SAID_LONGEST`, never inside a redaction mark: `said_json` (with `cut: true`), so every face that shows a result, and `said_line`, which escapes control characters. The packs needed no change: the web UI pack hands the page's whole text, which the handle now redacts whole | Keeping the 400-character bound and redacting inside `with_said`: `a2m-core` holds no redactor, and a pack's own redactor sees only its own secrets |

A finding on the way: a reconcile that stops with `cannot_reconcile` was queued again at once by the scheduler, because the run still holds a record in doubt, and read the system about every ten milliseconds until the caller's wait ran out. The runner now ends such a job as resting: the run stays `stopped`, with its failure, until someone resumes it.

## Consequences

- No first-party manifest lists `absence_steps`, so a record of identity `none` in doubt that its read-back does not find stops for a person in every pack. A pack whose lookup can state it adds the line to its manifest, with a test against its stand.
- A run whose binding changed between preview and write stops with `approval_stale`; a new preview and approval are needed. Failover after approval is gone for writes.
- The approval page of the local engine should show `PreviewRecord::binding_later` and `bindings`; until then it shows `not_read_back`, which carries the warning.
- `said` in a run's failure is stored up to 64 KiB, in the events and in the result's source; a result shows at most 400 characters of it.

## Revisit if

A lease long enough for a settle time of minutes is common, or a provider's lookup is authoritative only within a window it can name: then `absence_steps` takes a window.

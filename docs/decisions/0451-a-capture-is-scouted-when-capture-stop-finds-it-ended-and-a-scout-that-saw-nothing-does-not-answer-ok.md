# 0451. A capture is scouted when `capture.stop` finds it ended, and a scout that saw nothing does not answer `ok`

**Status:** Accepted. Design decision, made by the worker who built package E5, under the lead's brief. The owner had not read this record when it was merged. It changes no public interface: `capture.stop` gains `wait_seconds` and a `report` in its answer, and `scout.report` gains `scout`, all by addition.

**Date:** 2026-11-25

**Changes:** [`a2m-actions`](../architecture/crates/a2m-actions.md), whose `capture.stop` now runs the scout's stages over what the capture found (`scout_capture` in `handlers/scout.rs`), whose scout report says when it came from a capture, and whose `scout.run` answers with a failure when it saw nothing; the [action catalog](../reference/action-catalog.md). It builds [decision 0400](0400-a-capture-ends-as-a-scout-does.md) on the capture of [decision 0433](0433-a-capture-is-asked-for-by-capture-start-begun-by-its-person-on-the-page-and-held-in-the-engines-memory.md), and settles what they left to the builder. Point 10 of [decision 0442](0442-a-persons-use-is-watched-by-web-use-and-what-it-saw-twice-is-drafted-held.md), that drafting from a capture has no command, no longer holds. It answers finding 56 of field test 9 ([field tests](../reviews/field-tests.md)).

## Context

Decision 0400 says that a capture's findings enter the scout's pipeline at the draft stage, that `capture.stop` answers with the report's reference, and that the report says it came from a capture, with how long it ran, what it saw, and each bound it hit. It does not say which call runs those stages, since a capture may end on its page or at its bound with no call in hand; where the report keeps what changes from one capture to the next; or how a report is found by its reference. Field test 9 found that `scout.run` answered `ok` when its only surface's discovery failed (finding 56).

## Decision

1. **`capture.stop` runs the stages.** A capture that its person began and that has ended goes through the scout's stages in the first `capture.stop` that finds it ended, whoever or whatever ended it. The stages need a call: the caller's person, the project the drafts are written in, and the wait for each trial. A capture that ended on its page or at its bound has none until its asker calls `capture.stop`, which the capture already asks for next. A capture stopped before its person began it recorded nothing, and is not scouted.
2. **The capture's scout is a scout.** Its row is in `actions_scouts`, claimed for the capture when its first `capture.stop` finds it ended and kept with the capture, so that a second `capture.stop` gives the same answer. Its stage 0 says `from: capture` and keeps the capture: its identifier, how it ended, when it began and ended, the bounds its discoverers hit, and its findings counted by kind. Its stage 1 is the capture's surface with `observed_by: person`, what each discoverer reported, and the capture's identifier where a scout keeps its discovery run, since the capture's discoverers reported under it (decision 0433, point 4). From stage 2 on it is the scout's own code, `go_on` in `handlers/scout.rs`, shared with `scout.run`. A capture's scout left unfinished, because the call's wait ended before every trial was made, goes on with `capture.stop` again or with `scout.run`.
3. **What a capture's report says, and what its hash covers.** The report's `content` gains `capture`: how it ended, the bounds hit, the findings by kind (for a web application, its screens and its requests), and `coverage: partial`; and "not looked at" names what its person did not use. The capture's identifier, when it ran, and for how long are kept beside `content`, as the scout's identifier and its runs are, so that the same findings, captured again, give the same report by hash. The kernel names no kind of finding: "screens and requests" are the web pack's kinds, counted as any other.
4. **The reference.** `capture.stop` answers with `report`: the scout's identifier, `state` `finished` with the report's hash, or `working` with the stage it stopped at, and `scout.report` next with `scout` set to it. `scout.report` takes `scout`, to read that scout of the caller's person, of the target, in the call's project; without it, the newest finished, as before. `capture.stop` takes `wait_seconds` for its trials, as `scout.run` does, and carries the failure and the warnings the scout answers with.
5. **A scout that saw nothing does not answer `ok`** (finding 56). A surface the scout observed, one whose discovery ran, failed when its run failed or was refused, or when every discoverer of it failed and it found nothing. A surface with no provider or no discoverer was not observed, and counts for neither. When every observed surface failed, `scout.run` answers `ok: false` with the report beside the error, as a run that failed does: the error is the first such surface's own class and code, with the fix its discoverer's failure declares where the report keeps one, else to read `not_looked_at`. When some observed surface failed and another did not, it answers `ok`, with a warning naming each that failed. `scout.report` reads a stored report, and that succeeds: it gives the same warnings and no failure.
6. **An asserted read from a capture.** Decision 0400, point 3, has the report say of each asserted read that it cannot be tried under the drafts. A capture drafts none: the web pack's drafter writes no `read_asserted`, and a read by a method that may write is held with its effect asked (decision 0442, point 6). Every such draft is in the report as held, with its question, and is not tried.
7. **`endpoints.infer` stays without a handler.** Decision 0400 keeps it for running the inference again over stored findings; package E5's proof does not need it, and nothing calls it.

## Alternatives

| Alternative | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Run the stages in the capture's own job, as it ends | The job has no call: no project to draft in, no caller's person, and no wait. Keeping the start's call for later would act for a call that has already been answered |
| A new failure code of `a2m-actions`, such as one that says the scout saw nothing, for finding 56 | It would hide the class and code that say why: a browser that cannot start is `browser_not_owned`, and an agent that reads only the error should be told that |
| A failure for `scout.report` too, when its scout saw nothing | Reading a stored report succeeds; the warning tells the reader what `scout.run` told the one who ran it |
| Keep the capture's identifier and times in `content` | Two captures of the same use would never give the same report, by hash, which decision 0400 asks for |
| Prove E5 through the product with the first-party web packs | Every draft from a capture of a web application is held (decision 0442, point 8), so nothing would be tried. The proof is in `a2m-actions`'s tests, with the test pack whose rules draft a read; the product's `capture_use.rs` reads the report the web packs made through `capture stop` and `scout report` |

## Proof

`a_capture_ends_as_a_scout_does_and_its_report_is_the_same_twice`, in `crates/a2m-actions/tests/build.rs`: the stand's discoverer a person drives hands over three calls; an agent asks with `capture.start`, its person begins it on the page, and `capture.stop` ends it and answers with the report's reference, which `scout.report` reads by `scout`. The report says it came from a capture, ended `asked`, with `coverage: partial` and the findings by kind; the read is drafted and proven in five trials; the call that does not say whether it writes is held with its question; the call that writes is refused by the drafter and not tried; no step that saves reaches the stand; `capture.stop` again gives the same answer; and a second capture of the same findings gives another scout and the same report hash. Finding 56: `a_scout_whose_every_surface_failed_does_not_answer_ok`, in the same file, and `a_scout_answers_ok_only_when_a_surface_it_observed_did_not_fail`, in `handlers/scout.rs`. Through the product: `capture.rs` and `capture_use.rs`, whose drafts held from a person's use of the test application are now the scout's, read with `scout report`.

## Revisit if

A capture must be scouted while no one calls `capture.stop`, such as one ended on its page whose asker has gone; or a person surface other than the engine's page ends captures and must be told the report.
